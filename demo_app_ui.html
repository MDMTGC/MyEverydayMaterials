Black Plastic
Takeout Containers

The hidden recycling problem in your kitchen and why to avoid microwaving them.

Caution
Transfer to Glass

Black plastic takeout containers are notoriously difficult to recycle because sorting lasers cannot detect the carbon black pigment. To cut costs, many manufacturers use recycled plastic from electronic waste (like TV casings) which contains harmful brominated flame retardants (BFRs).

The Heath Risks

🔋
Electronic Waste Contamination

Studies have found concerning levels of heavy metals and brominated flame retardants (BFRs) in black plastic food containers, originating from unregulated recycled e-waste.

🧬
Endocrine Disruption

BFRs are known endocrine disruptors that can interfere with thyroid function and have been linked to developmental and reproductive issues.

🔥
Heat Extraction

Microwaving black plastic accelerates the leaching of these toxic chemicals directly into your hot food.
