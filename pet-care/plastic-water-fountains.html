Why Fountains Accumulate Contamination

Continuously running water in a warm home creates ideal conditions for biofilm — a structured community of bacteria, including Serratia and Pseudomonas — to colonize the inner surfaces of plastic fountain reservoirs.

The Health Risks

  • Chronic UTIs and Kidney Issues: Bacterial biofilm is a documented contributor to recurrent urinary tract infections and chronic kidney disease in cats — conditions that are already prevalent in the species.
  • BPA Leaching: Most plastic fountains use polycarbonate or ABS plastic. Heat from pump motors and UV light from windows accelerates BPA migration into drinking water.
  • Mold in Filters: Activated carbon filters in these fountains can harbor mold within 2–4 weeks if not replaced on schedule.

What You Can Do Right Now

  • Weekly Disassembly: Fully disassemble and scrub every surface weekly — not just rinse. Biofilm is resistant to simple rinsing.
  • Filter Replacement: Replace carbon filters every 2–4 weeks, not the manufacturer's suggested 4–8 weeks.
  • Upgrade Material: Stainless steel or glazed ceramic fountains are non-porous and significantly easier to keep biofilm-free.
