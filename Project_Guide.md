# Project: MyEverydayMaterials (2026)

## 🎯 Vision & Goals
- **Mission:** Provide authoritative, science-backed safety data for common household materials.
- **Revenue Goal:** Automate a high-volume, low-maintenance site for passive income ($1k+/mo target).
- **First Milestone:** Build and deploy the first 50 unique, high-quality articles.

## 🛠 Tech Stack & Autonomy
- **Framework:** Claude Code has full authority to select the optimal 2026 web stack (Astro, Hugo, or Enhanced Vanilla) to maximize performance and mobile-first SEO.
- **Deployment:** GitHub-linked repository (MDMTGC/MyEverydayMaterials) hosted on Netlify (myeverydaymaterials.com).
- **Automation:** Use Python or Shell scripts to handle bulk research and content injection.

## 🎨 Design & UX Directives
- **Aesthetic:** Modern Minimalism; "Technical Journal" vibe; High-readability typography (Inter/System-ui).
- **Mobile-First:** Perfectly responsive; high-visibility "30-Second Verdict" box for every article.
- **Integrity Disclosure:** Place this exact snippet at the top of every article:
  > "**Note from the Editor:** At Everyday Materials, our goal is to help you navigate the science of your home. We only recommend 'Better Alternatives' that we’ve researched extensively and would feel safe using in our own kitchens and lives. If you purchase through one of our links, we may earn a small commission from Amazon at no extra cost to you. This helps us keep the lights on and the research coming. Thank you for trusting us."

## 🛒 Product Criteria (The "Would We Use It?" Standard)
When selecting Amazon Associate products for the "Better Alternatives" section, Claude must verify:
- **Material Purity:** No BPA, PFAS, Phthalates, or Lead.
- **Practical Utility:** Must be highly-rated (4.2+ stars) and functional for everyday use.
- **Contextual Fit:** Prioritize products like glass storage, ceramic-coated cookware, and natural-fiber textiles.

## 🌐 Browser Automation & Troubleshooting
- **Live Verification:** Open `myeverydaymaterials.com` after pushes to verify propagation.
- **Visual Testing:** Check screenshots for mobile layout consistency.
- **Troubleshooting:** Visit Netlify dashboard to clear cache or check build logs if updates lag.
- **Research Sources:** Use NIH/PubMed, EPA, EWG, and ScienceDirect.

## 🧪 Research & Build Process
1. **Research:** Use browser access to find current (2026) safety data for items in `materials_data.json`.
2. **Monetization:** Automatically identify the best "Safe Alternative" for each material and insert your Amazon Associates link.
3. **SEO:** Mark all affiliate links as `rel="sponsored nofollow"`.

## 📂 The First 50 Articles (Categorized Roadmap)
- **Kitchen:** PFAS, BPA, Phthalates, Melamine, Teflon (PTFE), Black Plastic, Bamboo Fiber, Silicone, Lead Glaze.
- **Metals:** Aluminum Foil, Cast Iron (Seasoning), Unlined Copper, Antimony (PET), Lightbulb Mercury.
- **Home:** Formaldehyde (MDF), Flame Retardants, VOCs, Asbestos, Polyurethane, PVC Flooring, Radon, Fiberglass.
- **Personal Care:** Talc, Parabens, Triclosan, Fragrance, Methylene Glycol, SLS, Aluminum, PPD, Titanium Dioxide.
- **Cleaning:** Chlorine Bleach, Quats, Ammonia, 2-Butoxyethanol, Caustic Soda, Phosphates, Pesticide Residue.
- **Fabrics:** Microplastics (Laundry), Azo Dyes, Perchloroethylene (Dry Cleaning).
