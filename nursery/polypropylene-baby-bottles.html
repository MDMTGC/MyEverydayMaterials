The Heat-Shedding Mechanism

Polypropylene (PP) is the most common plastic for baby bottles.
  • Exposure: Shaking hot liquids in these bottles significantly increases the release of microplastics and nanoplastics.
  • Mitigation: Sterilize with heat, but allow the bottle to cool completely before adding formula or breast milk.
