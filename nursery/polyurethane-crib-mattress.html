Sleeping in VOCs

Polyurethane foam is a major contributor to indoor Volatile Organic Compound (VOC) levels.
  • Inhalation: Babies breathe at a higher rate than adults, increasing their relative intake of off-gassed chemicals during sleep.
  • Action: Choose mattresses with **Greenguard Gold** or **GOTS** certifications to ensure low emissions.
