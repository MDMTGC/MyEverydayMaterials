The Chemical Balance of Moisture

Baby wipes require potent preservatives to prevent mold and bacterial growth in their moist environment.
  • Skin Barrier Sensitivity: Infants have a thinner skin barrier than adults, making them more susceptible to chemical absorption and irritation from preservatives like MIT.
  • Action: Choose wipes that are 'Water-Based' (99% water) and free from added fragrances or alcohol.
