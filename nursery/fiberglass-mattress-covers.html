The Hidden Fire Barrier

Fiberglass is a cheap fire-retardant material. If the outer cover is removed or wears thin, microscopic glass shards can be released.
  • Health Risk: Inhalation of fiberglass particles causes severe respiratory irritation and persistent skin itching.
  • Verification: Check the label for 'glass fiber' or 'silica.' If found, never remove the mattress cover.
