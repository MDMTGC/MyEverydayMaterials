AVOID — Soft foam mats made of PVC contain phthalates and may emit formamide; prioritize natural rubber or TPU mats.

Crawling Hazards

Infants spend hours in direct skin contact with play mats.
  • Chemical Migration: Phthalates used to soften the PVC can migrate to the baby's skin or into household dust.
  • Action: Look for mats labeled 'PVC-Free' or made from food-grade TPU (Thermoplastic Polyurethane).
