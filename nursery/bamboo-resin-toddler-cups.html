The 'Natural' Illusion

Many bamboo cups are not solid wood but pulverized bamboo fibers bonded with plastic resins.
  • Leaching Risks: When exposed to high heat (microwaves or hot milk), the resin can release melamine and formaldehyde into the food.
  • Alternative: Use 18/8 stainless steel or tempered glass for toddler drinking vessels.
