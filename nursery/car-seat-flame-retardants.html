Regulatory Fire Safety

Federal standards (FMVSS 302) often lead manufacturers to treat car seat materials with chemical flame retardants.
  • Accumulation: Because cars are enclosed spaces that can heat up significantly, these chemicals can off-gas into the interior air.
  • Action: Look for brands that offer 'FR-Free' models, which use naturally fire-resistant wool or tight-weave fabrics to meet standards without chemicals.
