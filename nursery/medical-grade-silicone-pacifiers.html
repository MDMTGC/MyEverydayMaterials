SAFE — Medical-grade silicone is generally the safest option for pacifiers as it is heat-stable and less likely to harbor bacteria.

Stable Polymers

Silicone does not require plasticizers like phthalates to remain flexible.
  • Latex Allergy: While natural rubber (latex) is an alternative, it can trigger allergies in some infants and degrades faster than silicone.
  • Maintenance: Inspect silicone pacifiers daily for tears; once the surface is breached, it can become a breeding ground for bacteria.
