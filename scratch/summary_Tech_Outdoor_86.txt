### What are Rare Earth Elements?
Rare earth elements are a group of 17 chemical elements—the 15 lanthanides plus scandium and yttrium—that are essential for many modern technologies[3][1][4]. Despite the name "rare," they are relatively abundant in the Earth's crust, but they are rarely found in concentrations high enough to make mining economically viable[3][1][5]. 

They are highly valued for their unique:
*   **Magnetic Properties:** Critical for permanent magnets used in electric vehicles (EVs), wind turbines, and defense systems[1][5][2].
*   **Luminescent & Catalytic Properties:** Used in electronics, medical technology, and pollution-control devices like catalytic converters[3][1][5][4].
