### Key Points Typically Covered in AAP Guidance:
*   **Purpose:** Pacifiers can help satisfy a baby's natural urge to suck for comfort when they are not hungry[1][3][5].
*   **Breastfeeding:** For breastfed infants, the AAP generally recommends waiting until breastfeeding is well-established (often 3 to 4 weeks) before introducing a pacifier to avoid nipple confusion[2][3][6][5].
*   **SIDS Risk:** Offering a pacifier at nap time and bedtime has been associated with a reduced risk of Sudden Infant Death Syndrome (SIDS)[1][3][7][4].
*   **Safety Tips:**
    *   Use a one-piece model to prevent choking hazards[1][7][8].
    *   Never tie a pacifier around a baby's neck or to their clothing/crib[1][9][7].
    *   Keep the pacifier clean and replace it if it becomes damaged or worn[2][9][7].
    *   Do not force a baby to take a pacifier if they do not want it[1][6][7].
