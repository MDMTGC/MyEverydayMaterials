Cosmetics Europe has published a specific recommendation regarding the use of mineral hydrocarbons in cosmetic products, particularly those intended for lip care[1][2][3].

### **Cosmetics Europe Recommendation N°14**
*   **Title:** Mineral hydrocarbons in cosmetic lip care products[1][3]
*   **Date:** September 17, 2018 (updated from March 2013)[1]
*   **Key Recommendation:** Cosmetics Europe recommends that only mineral hydrocarbon fractions for which an Acceptable Daily Intake (ADI) has been established should be used in cosmetic lip care products[1][4]. This is to ensure safety regarding potential oral exposure[1][4].
*   **Scope:** This applies to all mineral oil hydrocarbons (including mineral oils and microcrystalline waxes) used as raw materials in lip care products (e.g., lipsticks, lip gloss, lip balm)[1]. It does not apply to synthetic hydrocarbons that are not complex mixtures[1].
*   **Safety Basis:** The recommendation references EFSA’s establishment of an ADI of 12 mg/kg bw/day for high and medium viscosity white mineral oils[1][4]. It emphasizes compliance with purity specifications for polycyclic aromatic hydrocarbons (PAH) as reflected in various pharmacopeia and EU Cosmetic Regulation (EC) No 1223/2009[1][4].
